======================================
OPERATION NEPTUNE: TERMINAL OF DESPINA
======================================

To the engineer receiving this signal,

A freezing storm on Neptune is approaching the Despina research station.
The cooling system has failed, and the station needs your help.

You can now control the system only through the command-line interface.

Your mission is to:

  1. Explore the station and find the hidden signal.
  2. Find errors in the station logs.
  3. Search for clues about the cooling system.
  4. Restore the system and make the station operational again.
  5. Record your dream in the Dream Capsule.

First clue:
The station logs are in the logs folder
inside the ~/neptune home directory.

Before setting out, take another look around.
A signal may be hidden somewhere close to you.

Good luck, engineer.
